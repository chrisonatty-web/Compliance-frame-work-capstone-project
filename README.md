## Project Overview

This project focuses on developing an **ISO/IEC 27001-aligned Information Security Management System (ISMS)** for PayNest Technologies, a growing Nigerian FinTech startup that provides digital payment services to individuals and small businesses.

As the organisation expands, it processes increasing volumes of sensitive information, including customer business information, transaction data, employee records, financial information, application source code, and cloud-based systems.

To strengthen its information security posture and support its certification readiness, the organisation requires a structured approach to information security governance, risk management, security controls, and regulatory compliance.

This capstone project examines the organisation's existing information security weaknesses, identifies gaps against relevant ISO/IEC 27001 requirements, considers associated risks, and recommends improvements to establish a more effective ISMS.

## Problem Statement

PayNest Technologies faces several information security and governance challenges that could affect its ability to protect sensitive information, maintain business operations, and achieve its organisational objectives.

The assessment identified the following weaknesses:

- No clearly defined ISMS scope.
- Incomplete information security policies.
- No formal information asset inventory.
- Inconsistent information security risk assessments.
- Security controls have not been formally selected.
- No documented Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- Irregular information security awareness training.
- Inconsistent third-party security assessments.

These weaknesses create potential exposure to information security incidents, unauthorised access, data breaches, inconsistent risk management, and difficulties demonstrating compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 requirements.

The project therefore proposes a structured compliance improvement programme to address these gaps and strengthen the organisation's information security management practices.

## Key Areas of Assessment

### 1. ISMS Scope and Organisational Context

**Identified gap:**
PayNest Technologies does not have a clearly defined ISMS scope.

**Relevant ISO/IEC 27001 requirement:**
- Clause 4.1 – Understanding the organisation and its context.

**Recommended improvement:**
Define the ISMS scope, establish organisational context, and clarify roles, responsibilities, and accountability across relevant business areas.

### 2. Information Security Policies

**Identified gap:**
Existing security policies are incomplete and do not adequately address relevant information security requirements, responsibilities, and operational practices.

**Relevant ISO/IEC 27001 requirement:**
- Clause 5.2 – Information security policy.

**Recommended improvement:**
Establish, approve, and maintain an appropriate information security policy that provides direction for protecting organisational information and supporting information security objectives.

### 3. Information Security Awareness and Training

**Identified gap:**
Security awareness training is irregular, with no consistent training schedule or completion records.

**Relevant ISO/IEC 27001 requirement:**
- Clause 6.3 – Planning of changes.
- Information security awareness, education and training is also identified as an important improvement area in the project.

**Recommended improvement:**
Introduce regular information security awareness exercises, maintain training records, and review employee awareness to improve their ability to identify and respond to information security threats.

### 4. Information Asset Management

**Identified gap:**
The organisation lacks a formal, documented, and consistently maintained inventory of information and associated assets.

**Relevant ISO/IEC 27001 control:**
- Control 5.9 – Inventory of information and other associated assets.

**Recommended improvement:**
Implement and maintain a documented information asset inventory that includes:

- Asset identification.
- Defined asset ownership.
- Information classification.
- Appropriate asset management responsibilities.
- Periodic inventory reviews.
- Identification and removal of obsolete assets.

### 5. Security Control Selection and Statement of Applicability

**Identified gap:**
Security controls have not been formally selected, and a Statement of Applicability (SoA) has not been established.

**Project recommendation:**
Select appropriate information security controls based on identified risks and document their applicability in a Statement of Applicability.

The SoA should explain the selected controls, their justification, and their implementation status.

### 6. Third-Party and Supplier Security Management

**Identified gap:**
Supplier security assessments are performed inconsistently, creating potential exposure where third parties access organisational information without adequate assessment.

**Relevant ISO/IEC 27001 control:**
- Control 5.20 – Addressing information security within supplier agreements.

**Recommended improvement:**
Implement a documented supplier security management process that includes:

- Supplier risk classification.
- Information security requirements in supplier agreements.
- Supplier security assessments.
- Ongoing supplier monitoring.
- Tracking and remediation of identified security issues.

## Understanding the ISO/IEC 27001 Approach

ISO/IEC 27001 provides a framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System.

The project recognises that effective information security management requires more than simply creating policies and procedures. Organisations must demonstrate that their security controls operate effectively and that information security risks are managed consistently.

The project also highlights the importance of the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) approach to continual improvement.

The four stages are:

1. **Plan:** Establish the ISMS, understand organisational context, identify risks, and determine security objectives.
2. **Do:** Implement the selected security controls and improvement activities.
3. **Check:** Monitor, assess, and evaluate the effectiveness of the ISMS.
4. **Act:** Address identified weaknesses and continually improve the information security management system.

## Limitations and Project Scope

This capstone project presents an assessment and recommended improvement approach based on the information provided about PayNest Technologies.

The supplied documentation does not establish that the proposed controls have been implemented, independently tested, or formally audited.

It should therefore be understood as an ISO/IEC 27001-aligned compliance improvement and certification-readiness project, not evidence of ISO/IEC 27001 certification.

Additional implementation evidence, detailed risk registers, an approved ISMS scope, and a completed Statement of Applicability would strengthen the assessment.
